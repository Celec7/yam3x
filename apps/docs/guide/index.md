# Guides

Use these guides to get started and maintain the component source copied into your application.

## Get started

1. Follow [Installation and first use](/guide/installation) to set up your Vue/Vite project and add components.
2. Browse examples, APIs, and Material guidance in the [component catalog](/components/).
3. Adapt the copied component source to your application.

## Maintain your app

- [Update copied source](/guide/updating): compare versions and merge changes manually.

Design guidance for theming and interactions is grouped under [Design foundations](/guide/design).
